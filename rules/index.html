Community rules

Keep it fair, keep it friendly

These are the basics. The complete rules live in our Discord, and staff may act on anything that harms other players.

No Unfair Advantages

No hacked clients, no prohibited modifications, and no exploiting bugs for an edge.

Respect the Community

No harassment, threats, or deliberately disrupting other players.

No Toxic Griefing

Don't grief outside areas where a gamemode explicitly allows it.

Follow Staff Instructions

Follow staff in the moment. If you think a decision was wrong, you can submit an appeal afterwards.
